# Product Roadmap and Future Enhancements

The following details features planned for upcoming releases:

---

## 1. Digital Telemedicine chamber
- **WebRTC integration**: Allow doctor consultation sessions over live video feeds.
- **In-app chat room**: Share patient medical history files securely.

---

## 2. Dynamic Appointment scheduling
- **Time slot bookings**: Pre-book doctor appointments online.
- **Calendar sync**: Sync booking details with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.

---

## 3. Stripe Billing & Subscriptions
- **Clinic subscription tiers**: Charge clinics monthly based on their doctor count or active tokens volume.
- **Direct copay payments**: Allow patients to pay doctor fees online during queue check-in.

---

## 4. AI Queue predictions & Voice Calls
- **Consultation duration forecasts**: Use machine learning to analyze doctor history and predict patient wait times.
- **Lobby loudspeaker calls**: Integrate voice calling to read ticket numbers over lobby speakers.
